Before Dawn

Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026. This policy covers the Before Dawn: Prayer Lock app for iPhone (“Before Dawn”), published by Patra Labs (“we”).

The short version

Before Dawn does not collect your data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no server that receives your information. What you write and the apps you choose stay on your iPhone.

Information stored on your device

  • Your answers and preferences: the first name you enter (optional), your onboarding answers, the Scripture theme you choose, and your commitment prayer date.
  • Your prayer history: when you complete a prayer pause and how long it took, used for your streak and weekly Sabbath report.
  • Your journal: reflections you choose to save. Prayers typed during a prayer pause are not saved.
  • Your app choices: Apple’s Screen Time picker gives Before Dawn anonymous tokens for the apps, categories and websites you select. We cannot see which apps they are. The tokens are kept in the app’s private storage on your device so the prayer pause can appear.

This information never leaves your device through Before Dawn. Your device backups (for example iCloud Backup) may include it, under Apple’s policies.

On-device reflections

On supported iPhones, optional journal reflections are generated by Apple’s on-device language model. Your journal text is processed on your iPhone and is not sent to us or to any third-party service.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Before Dawn schedules local notifications on your device: a reminder before a free trial ends, and the “Pray first” prompt that opens a prayer pause. No notification content is sent to a server.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether a subscription is active, under Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Children

Before Dawn is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Your choices

You can turn off the prayer pause, revoke Screen Time access in Settings › Screen Time, turn off notifications in Settings, and delete all app data from Care › Delete my local data or by deleting the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
