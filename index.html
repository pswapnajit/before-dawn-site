How does the prayer pause work?

Before Dawn uses Apple’s Screen Time framework. When you open an app you chose, a screen asks you to pray first. Tap Pray first, then the notification, to open Before Dawn. Read a verse or pray, hold to say Amen, and choose a 5, 10 or 15 minute window. Your prayer pause comes back when the window ends.

Which apps can see my choices?

None, including us. Apple’s Screen Time picker gives Before Dawn only anonymous tokens, and they stay on your iPhone.

How do I turn the prayer pause off?

Open Before Dawn, go to Rhythm and switch off Prayer before scrolling. You can also remove Screen Time access in Settings › Screen Time. Turning it off never requires a subscription.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Before Dawn. Cancel at least 24 hours before your trial or current period ends to avoid the next charge. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I bought a subscription on another device

Tap Restore purchases on the plans screen, or in Care inside the app, while signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I delete my data?

Your prayers, journal and settings are stored only on your device. In the app, open Care and tap Delete my local data, or delete the app. Deleting data does not cancel a subscription. Your anonymous subscription record is kept by our subscription service, RevenueCat; email us to have it deleted.
